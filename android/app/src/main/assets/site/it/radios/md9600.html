Radio

TYT MD-9600

Supportata

One image serves every board revision. On a V1-V3 radio, FM and DMR on both bands, DMR voice and data, text messages, roaming, scan, dual watch, APRS, the hotspot, each split duplex half and the USB network services work on air, with every power level checked on a wattmeter on both bands. V4A/V5 boards are detected; on older boards the radio asks for the revision once. Open: V4 and V4A/V5 boards have not run on a radio, and SFR loses inbound voice frames.

Entrare in modalità aggiornamento

  1. Disconnect the radio's DC power cable (the power key alone is not enough) and connect the USB cable to the mini-USB port on the rear.
  2. Hold P1 and the orange (alarm) key while you connect the power again. The screen stays blank and the backlight blinks once a second.
  3. Release the keys.
