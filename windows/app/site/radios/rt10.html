Boots from the boot ROM with the host link, stub entry, the watchdog and the crash log. FM receive with CTCSS/DCS decode, FM transmit, DMR transmit and receive with the radio's own codec, P25 (conventional, and trunked transmit) and NXDN at 4800 bit/s on stored channels, and APRS transmit work on the radio.

Entering Update Mode

  1. Connect the programming cable to the radio and to the computer.
  2. Open the radio programming utility and choose the cable. A radio that already runs RadioFoundry is detected without holding keys or turning it off.
  3. Otherwise, turn the radio off and on again when prompted. Do not hold any keys. The screen stays dark and the LED on top blinks yellow while the radio is in update mode.
