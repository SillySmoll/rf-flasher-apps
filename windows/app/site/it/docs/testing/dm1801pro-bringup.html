DM-1801 Pro bring-up checklist

The order to bring the DM-1801 Pro port (make BOARD=dm1801pro) up on a radio, what each step proves, and what to change when it doesn't work. Steps 0 to 3, 7 and 8 passed on 2026-10-15 (hardware-dm1801pro.md 12). Hardware facts: hardware-dm1801pro.md.

The feature checklist, with every feature's required HIL tests and the last recorded result, is tools/bringup.py list --radio dm1801pro. tools/bringup.py run --radio dm1801pro records a run (tests/hil/README.md). This page keeps the hardware steps and what to change when one fails.

0. Before anything

  1. Back up both flashes (1 MB program flash, 16 MB data flash). The port never writes the data flash below 0x700000 and never touches the boot loader, but a backup is the only way back from a mistake.
  2. Confirm update mode before the first flash: it is the recovery path for every step below. The loader stays in it while PTT and SK1 are held at power-on (hardware-dm1801pro.md 6.2). Push the cable fully into the jack, or the loader doesn't answer. python3 tools/serial_loader.py --model dm1801pro --dry-run IMAGE checks the image and the cable first.
  3. Flash: python3 tools/serial_loader.py --model dm1801pro build/dm1801pro/rfoundry_dm1801pro.bin over the programming cable. The erase takes about 13 s. rfoundry flash IMAGE --board dm1801pro wraps the same tool, and the stub for a radio that runs RadioFoundry with CONFIG_STUB_ENTRY=y (tools/rfoundry/README.md); neither rfoundry route has flashed a radio yet.
  4. The debug link runs on the same cable at 115200 (RFOUNDRY_PORT=/dev/cu.usbserial-... tools/rfoundry_debug.py diag). The cable's plug switches the internal speaker off: unplug it to listen.
  5. Build: make BOARD=dm1801pro (csky-elf GCC: tools/build-csky-toolchain.sh).

2. Keys

Open the Diagnostics page (Settings > Diagnostics). The keys line shows the raw scan: matrix bit = strobe x 4 + input (bits 0-19), the four direct pins in bits 20-23. Press every key in turn and write down the bit. Then fix, in input_dm1801pro.c: the map table (matrix keys) and direct_keys (PTT, SK1, SK2, SOS). Until then keys are mixed up.

3. Flash, calibration, battery

bat Rises with a full battery; the percentage is close The ADC divider isn't known: compute the scale from a multimeter and set it in power_dm1801pro.c

4. FM receive

Tune a known local signal on VHF and UHF. Audio, RSSI (rssi line) and squelch should follow the signal.

Symptom Likely cause
No audio on either band The cable is plugged in (it mutes the speaker); PIN_AMP_EN (PTA16) or PIN_SPK_SOURCE (PTB15)
Audio on one band only The path pins' band side is inverted (radio_dm1801pro.c path_pins; hardware-dm1801pro.md 5.5)
Wrong frequency or very weak fd6818_variant_b tables, the RF bus speed (RF_BIT_SCALE_US), PIN_RF_CS
Narrow / wide swapped The 12.5 kHz word (bw_fm_12k5, hardware-dm1801pro.md 5.2)

5. FM transmit

Only with a dummy load or a test set on an allowed frequency. The radio refuses to transmit when the calibration isn't read.

  1. Key at low power: the carrier is on frequency with no splatter. Check the PA ramp and the TX path pins.
  2. Compare the three power levels with a meter: L < M < H, the nominal values are hardware-dm1801pro.md 6.4's open item (which level uses which array).
  3. Deviation and modulation balance with a test set; the modulation byte is read at the calibration page's TX points (hardware-dm1801pro.md 6.4).
  4. Repeat 1 to 3 on VHF. The pa 145 line shows the VHF drive. No power on VHF while UHF works points at the TX path's VHF side (PTA31, hardware-dm1801pro.md 5.5).

5.1 FM mic gain

The FM mic gain is the board's level for the channel bandwidth (MIC_GAIN_FM_12K5_DB, MIC_GAIN_FM_25K_DB in boards/dm1801pro/radio_dm1801pro.c) plus the Mic gain setting's offset, (step - 5) x 2 dB (radio-features.md 3.6). The programming cable cuts the microphone, and no injected tone passes the mic gain, so every measurement is an over with the cable out. tests/hil/test_mic_gain.py runs the session: it sets the step and the bandwidth over the debug link, the SDR bench records while you key, and it reads the step back once the cable is in again.

python3 -m pytest tests/hil/test_mic_gain.py -v -rs -s --manual --allow-rf --fm-mhz MHZ --mic-gain-steps 3,5,7

For each over (3 steps at 12.5 kHz, then at 25 kHz):

  1. Wait for === over N/6: ... === and GO. The bench records for --mic-gain-window s (30).
  2. Unplug the cable, key PTT, give the same source each time for about 5 s (a steady whistle or a phone playing a 1 kHz tone at a fixed distance, or the same sentence), release PTT, plug the cable back in.
  3. The test prints the bench board's measurements and link back: step S, GCR_ADCL 0xNN.

At the end it prints one row per over: bandwidth, step, offset, GCR_ADCL (bits 5:0 = dB), segment length, voice peak (99.5 %) and rms deviation, and the strongest tone with its deviation. Expect the deviation to rise about 2 dB a step until the deviation limiter. Target with speech: peak about 2.5 kHz at 12.5 kHz and 5 kHz at 25 kHz. Set the two board levels from the step that reaches the target (level = 11 + offset), then rerun at step 5 to confirm.

If Check
GCR_ADCL doesn't follow the step set.mic_gain_board=1 in the features view; the FM transmit start writes the register (tx())
No keyed segment PTT pressed inside the window; the capture under ~/<bench dir>/hil_mic_gain_N/
The same deviation at every step the limiter (DEV_LIMITER) clips: try lower steps; or the gain isn't in the path the microphone takes

6. Broadcast FM and air band

6.1 Broadcast FM tuner

Tune the VFO into the broadcast band (airband-and-broadcast.md 4). tests/hil/test_listen_bands.py checks the view keys without keying.

Step Expect If not
VFO to 100.1 MHz bcast=1, bcast_fault=0 in the view: the chip answers at 0x11 with ID 0x58 bcast_fault=1: PIN_BCAST_EN polarity, PIN_BCAST_SDA / PIN_BCAST_SCL, the I2C speed (half_us), the reference clock mode (clk_mode), all in platform/hrc7000/bcast_hrc.c and the board's hrc_bcast_cfg_t
A local station Audible; the signal meter moves; the knob steps 200 kHz on the odd tenths The tuner's audio route; the amplifier (PIN_AMP_EN) is shared with the channel audio
Squelch 9 on a weak station Still plays (broadcast audio ignores the squelch); the green LED stays on The squelch threshold (RADIO_BCAST_SQL_REF)
Scan a zone of broadcast channels Stops on stations, quiet between them The squelch threshold
PTT "Broadcast: receive only", nothing keys The core's refusal

7. Accelerometer

The diagnostics page has an acc line: x, y and z in milli-g. Lay the radio flat: z reads about +1000 or -1000 and x and y near 0; tilt it and the values follow. accel none means the chip didn't answer: check PIN_ACCEL_SCL / PIN_ACCEL_SDA and the address (ACCEL_ADDR in accel_dm1801pro.c; 0x18 if the chip's address pin is low). accel id NN means the chip answered with an ID the driver doesn't accept (this radio's SC7A20 reads 0x11).

8. Vibration motor

The radio vibrates for about 0.3 s when it powers on. No pulse: put a meter on PTB24 (PIN_VIBRATION); if the pin moves and the motor doesn't, the pin isn't the motor driver (hardware-dm1801pro.md 8 is inferred, no label names it).

9. DMR

DMR is built (CONFIG_DMR), without voice. Do this after FM receive and transmit work. The Diagnostics page starts with the DMR counters.

Step Expect If not
Select a DMR channel on a local repeater or hotspot tk (ticks) climbs by about 33 a second, ts interrupts count up No ticks: the modem never got timing; check the DMR mode register set (dmr_table in drivers/rf/fd6818/fd6818_setup_b.c) and the RX path
Receive a transmission H (headers) and V / T counters rise, crc stays low Many crc or u (unusable): the bandwidth word 0x3048 or the DMR register set
Hear a call Silence: the codec is located but not hosted yet
Transmit on a dummy load (data call or a short key-up) A burst per slot on a meter or SDR, tx, rf and ok rise un (underrun) or rj: the per-burst switch takes too long (dmr_rf_switch, RF_BIT_SCALE_US); no carrier: dmr_pa_on, the 28 ms PA delay
The mic on DMR transmit, no person: pytest tests/hil -k mic_uart_inject --allow-rf --mic-inject on the host the cable is plugged into (its device path, not a network bridge) The tone bursts sent on the cable's line show at the vocoder: voice.tx_mic_spread_db 12 or more, tx_mic_level_db 9 dB over a quiet over Both overs at the ADC's floor (level about 6, no spread): the mic doesn't reach the codec ADC on DMR. PTB14 must be high, as for FM (hardware-dm1801pro.md 3.1)

9.1 Tier III receive (listen only)

Against a Tier III site on the SDR bench (TSCC on one carrier, a second traffic carrier), with the engine staged over the debug link listen-only (T3_OPT_LISTEN_ONLY) and tx_inhibit set, read from the t3 and dmr_stats views (dmr-trunking.md 11):

Step Seen If not
Start on the TSCC t3.state=camped within 0.4-0.7 s, t3.syscode the site's, t3.csbk rising at the site's CSBK rate, t3.crc_bad 0-1 in 400 No CSBKs: the CC, or the channel not in repeater mode (the TSCC is a BS signal)
Short LC t3.slc_ok rising 6-8 a second on the TSCC, t3.slc_bad under 1 % of them (a few more on a payload channel) slc_ok 0: system interrupt bit 4 not enabled, or the short LC read (hrc7000_dmr_short_lc) not called by the event task
Slot ticks t3.ticks about 33 a second, t3.at_known rising with them once the TC lock holds, t3.at_busy while the inbound is in use at_known 0: no TC lock (the channel not in repeater mode, or the TC not alternating)
Group call on the TSCC carrier's other slot followed in about 0.5-1 s (t3.state=follow, t3.slot=2), voice decoded (call.rx_voice_bursts about 49 of 50 a 3 s call), back on the TSCC (t3.returns)
Group call on the second carrier followed there (t3.rx_hz the carrier's, t3.slot=1), voice decoded, back on the TSCC

Not checked on the radio: an MBC continuation with the chip's CRC flag set (host test only) and the audio heard (the cable mutes the speaker; the counters show it decoded).

9.2 Tier III transmit

The same site, the engine staged over the debug link with transmit, the lowest power setting, the radio's ID registered (t3, t3_svc, dmr_ext, dmr_ip views). Every step starts the engine again, so each one registers first:

Step Seen If not
Register t3.reg=ok, t3.ax_reason=0x62, 1-4 random accesses (t3.ra_tries) t3.sent 0: the engine listen-only or tx_inhibit set
Group call, PTT with the test tone (voice_test) granted in about 0.3 s, t3.slot=2, keyed (state=tx), back on the TSCC (t3.returns) after the hang
Private call the same, t3.call_dst the called ID
Group and private call from another MS followed, 49 voice bursts a 3 s call, voice.rx_peak about 16700 (speech), back on the TSCC voice.rx_peak near 0: the other MS sent silence
Status, short data to another MS t3.ax_result=done, t3.ax_reason=0x44
Short data poll from another MS the poll ahoy (t3.ahoy_kind=6), the answer UDT sent (t3.last_tx_op=0x1b)
Radio check during the radio's own call the answer sent in the call (t3.last_tx_op=0x23), back on the TSCC
IP datagram to the gateway, unconfirmed a data grant (t3.call_data=1), ip.sent +1, ip.result=ok, back on the TSCC confirmed: ip.cnf_timeouts with no response from the site (the site sends none yet)
Power save, PowerSave_RQ 2 t3.ps_offset granted, t3.sleeps and t3.rx_offs rising, the receiver off about half the time (t3.rx_off_ms); a status, short data, a poll (answered) and a group call from another MS all reach the sleeping radio t3.rx_off_ms 0: the board's rx_sleep_dmr not set
Emergency: alarm, hot-mic call, cancel em.state=active, data.emergency_alarms_sent +1, em.hot_mic_keyed 1, a call followed and back on the TSCC, the cancel done

10. VOX and the microphone level

The level comes from the codec ADC's mic PCM (hardware-dm1801pro.md 12). The programming cable in the speaker/mic jack cuts the microphone: unplug it for the talking steps, or read the values from the diagnostics page.

Step Expect If not
FM channel, VOX off; read the features view twice (vox.level), then the diagnostics line mic lvl vox.mic=1; mic lvl ... on, and n grows by about 100 a second while the view is read off or n stuck: the PCM interrupt or AUDIO_CONTROL b4 (c7 ac line, bit 4)
Quiet room, then talk at the radio from 10 cm lvl and pk low in silence, well up while talking Flat while talking: the samples don't carry the microphone in receive (PTB14, the codec ADC mute, CR_ADC b7 in the cd lines)
VOX on (Quick tile, FM), dummy load, talk Keys on speech, drops vox_tail_ms after it Keys in silence: the noise floor is above the threshold; lower vox_level or check pk

11. After this

DMR voice needs the codec hosted (it is the DM-32UV's: hardware-dm1801pro.md 6.5) and the air band and the tuner a check on the radio (6). The open items are in hardware-dm1801pro.md 10.
