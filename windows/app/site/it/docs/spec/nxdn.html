NXDN

NXDN conventional voice (the NXDN Technical Specifications: TS 1-A Common Air Interface, TS 1-B Basic Operation), on the air interface (air-interface.md). Switch: CONFIG_NXDN, which also builds the air interface (build-config.md). Which radios could carry it: feature-matrix.md. Clause numbers below are TS 1-A's unless they say TS 1-B.

State:

Part State
Frame codec: FSW, LICH, scrambler, SACCH, FACCH1, the four VCH (1, 2) written, host tested
Receive, the call layer, the engine (3, 4, 7) written, host tested; 4800 bit/s, and 9600 bit/s with half-rate voice
Voice (5) through the DMR side's voice path to the vocoder the board hosts (hal_vocoder_t); host tested
The radio core: mode, settings, PTT, debug link, home screen (6) written, host tested
Codeplug channels, the site's Codeplug Editor (nxdn-codeplug.md) written, host tested
Scan (6.4) written, host tested; not checked on a radio
Dual watch written, host tested: a stored NXDN channel on either side, the receiver alternating onto it (radio-features.md 2.1); not checked on a radio
Channel editor on the radio written, host tested: RAN, rate, group, unit ID (ui.md 2.1)
On air transmit checked off air on a radio at both rates (test_nxdn_tx_off_air); receive checked at both rates with a bench transmitter, speech frames through the vocoder included (test_nxdn_rx_from_bench, test_nxdn_rx_speech); the sound at the speaker is not checked (8)
9600 bit/s full-rate voice (EFR), data, trunking, encryption not written (10). Encrypted calls are recognized and not played

1. On air

Item 4800 bit/s 9600 bit/s Clause
Channel 6.25 kHz 12.5 kHz table 2.3-1
Symbol rate 2400 symbols/s 4800 symbols/s table 2.3-1
Deviation of the outer symbols (+3, -3) 1050 Hz 2400 Hz table 3.3-1
Transmit filter root raised cosine, roll-off 0.2, times the shaper sin(πfT)/(πfT) same 3.4
Receive filter root raised cosine times the inverse shaper same 3.5

The dibit map is the air interface's (01 +3, 00 +1, 10 -1, 11 -3; 3.3).

A frame is 192 symbols (80 ms at 4800 bit/s, 40 ms at 9600):

Symbols Field Bits
0-9 Frame sync word (FSW), CDF59 20
10-17 LICH 16
18-47 SACCH 60
48-191 four VCH of 72 bits, or a FACCH1 in VCH 1-2 and/or 3-4 288

A transmission opens with a preamble: the reference deviation pattern (5F three times, 3.6), then 5775FD (4.4.3), 24 symbols. Every frame but the FSW is scrambled (4.6: X⁹ + X⁴ + 1, starting at 0 1 1 1 0 0 1 0 0, one bit per dibit's high bit).

The LICH (4.5.1, figure 4.5-7): seven bits (RF channel type 2, functional channel 2, steal option 2, direction 1) and even parity over the first four, each bit sent as an outer symbol (0 as 01, 1 as 11). A conventional voice channel's frames are RDCH (10).

Functional channel (USC) Meaning
00 SACCH non-superframe: a call's first and last frames
10 SACCH superframe: a call's voice frames; four make a superframe
11 SACCH superframe whose FACCH1 frame is idle (9600 bit/s, TS 1-B 4.1.2.1)
01 UDCH (data): refused
Steal option VCH 1-2 VCH 3-4
00 FACCH1 FACCH1
01 FACCH1 voice
10 voice FACCH1
11 voice voice

2. Fields and FEC (core/nxdn/nxdn_frame.c, core/fec/conv.c)

Field Content Code Clause
SACCH SR (structure 2 bits, RAN 6), 18 bits of message CRC6 (x⁶+x⁵+x²+x+1), 4 tail bits, rate 1/2 K=5, punctured to 60 bits (111111 / 110110), interleaved in rows of 12 4.5.3
FACCH1 one 80-bit layer 3 message CRC12 (x¹²+x¹¹+x³+x²+x+1), 4 tail bits, rate 1/2 K=5, punctured to 144 bits (11 / 01), interleaved in rows of 16 4.5.4
VCH 72 bits of voice with the voice frame's own FEC none on the frame level (5) 4.5.2, 7.2

The convolutional code is G1 = 1 + D³ + D⁴, G2 = 1 + D + D² + D⁴ (4.5.3). The CRCs start with all ones and are sent highest register bit first (figures 4.5-10, 4.5-11).

The Viterbi decoder is shared (core/fec/conv.c): any rate 1/2 code of constraint length 5, soft or hard, punctured bits as erasures. Its memory is the caller's: one uint16_t of decisions per input bit (192 bytes for a FACCH1), plus 64 bytes of path metrics and 32 of branch outputs on the stack (160 bytes of stack on Cortex-M4 at -Os). About 160 operations per input bit:

Decode Input bits Operations
SACCH 36 about 5,800
FACCH1 96 about 15,400
A voice frame (SACCH) about 5,800 per frame: 72,000/s at 4800 bit/s
A header or FACCH1 frame (SACCH, two FACCH1) about 36,500

Soft values come from the frame's soft symbols: a dibit's high bit by the sign, its low bit by the distance from the inner level. The free distance after puncturing is 5 (SACCH) and 4 (FACCH1).

3. Receive (core/nxdn/nxdn_rx.c)

The framer looks for two patterns:

Pattern Symbols Errors Where
FSW 10 2 bits every frame (5.3.1.2, N5)
Preamble tail and FSW 19 3 bits a transmission's start only (N6 = 1)

A 10-symbol pattern turns up in noise and in payload, so the head hook decides whether a frame is there:

Case Result
LICH parity holds, RDCH, not UDCH, at most one LICH symbol inner the frame is taken; the SACCH is decoded
SACCH CRC holds and its RAN is not ours (unless ours is 0) refused, counted (ran_rejects): another system on the channel
LICH lost inside a call's superframes up to two frames in a row are placed by the call's order
Five frames without a sync (N7) the signal is lost

The profile: 4-level, root raised cosine 0.2, the deviations of 1, bw 12.5 kHz (the narrowest a radio sets; 6.25 kHz is open, 9), and sync_any_syms = 192: a sync opens every frame, though none is 24 symbols long (air-interface.md 2).

4. The call layer (core/nxdn/nxdn_call.c)

Messages (TS 1-B 6.4): VCALL 01 and TX_REL 08 carry the same octets (type, control option, call type and voice option, source ID, destination ID, cipher and key ID in VCALL only). VCALL_IV 03 carries an encrypted call's initialization vector. IDs are 16 bits; FFFF is all.

Receive:

Frame Effect
Header (non-superframe, two FACCH1 VCALL) a call starts with its fields
A superframe frame without a call a call starts as a late entry; its fields come once the four SACCH parts (structures 1/4 to 4/4) are in, in order (TS 1-B 4.1.6)
VCALL_IV in either FACCH1 the call is encrypted
Header VCALL with other fields during a call the old call ended unseen: a new one starts
TX_REL, or the signal lost the call ends

Voice is handed on only once the VCALL is in, the cipher is none, no VCALL_IV was seen and the voice option is half rate (4800 or 9600 EHR). Everything else is counted (voice_held), not played.

Transmit (TS 1-B 4.1.3): preamble, a header frame (SACCH IDLE, VCALL in both FACCH1), superframe frames (SACCH VCALL part 1/4 to 4/4, four VCH), and a release frame (SACCH IDLE, TX_REL in both FACCH1). Frames go out symbol by symbol: a voice frame's FSW, LICH, SACCH and first VCH leave with its first voice frame, each later voice frame releases the next 36 symbols. At 9600 bit/s every second frame is a FACCH1 frame (TS 1-B 4.1.2.1). When the key is let go, the frame in hand is filled with silence; a frame with nothing on air yet is dropped and the release frame follows the one before. The direction bit is inbound (table 5.2-2).

5. Voice

A VCH is one 20 ms voice frame of the half-rate vocoder (3600 bit/s with FEC, 2450 without; 7.2), 72 bits. NXDN carries it scrambled with the frame (2) and otherwise as the vocoder gives it: its FEC is the vocoder's. The standard's two FEC test frames (7.2.1) give the same 72 bits through the DMR side's frame FEC (nxdnTest), so the frame is the one the board's DMR vocoder takes.

The voice path is the DMR side's (core/dmr/ambe/ambeVoice.c, dmr-voice.md): the engine's rx_voice hook hands each 72-bit frame to its sink, which takes the FEC off (ambeFec, 72 to 49 bits) and queues the 49 parameter bits for hal_vocoder_t; tx_voice takes the next frame from its source, which encodes the microphone's 49 bits and adds the FEC. The audio stream runs while the engine does. Nothing in the tree encodes or decodes NXDN speech: the engine, the call layer, the tests and nxdntool move made-up patterns.

Item State
Bit order settled: the standard's FEC test frames (7.2.1) are what ambeFecEncode gives for their parameter bits (nxdnTest), so the VCH is the DMR frame bit for bit
Silence the DMR side's silence frame (dmr_ambe_silence), which is 7.2.1's second test frame
9600 bit/s full rate (EFR, 144-bit VCH, 7200 bit/s) out of scope: such calls are held, not played

6. The radio core (core/radio/radio_nxdn.c)

RADIO_MODE_NXDN runs the engine on the board's air interface with the board in its digital mode and the DMR stack stopped, as P25 does (p25.md 7). The engine's memory is its own, or with P25 built and no lending vocoder the memory P25 keeps for itself (one engine runs at a time).

State Engine PTT
NXDN, the provider's fit has TX at the rate running keys: preamble, header, voice, release frame
NXDN, the fit has TX only running, nothing is received keys
NXDN, the fit has RX only running, receive only refused, TXDENY_AIR_NO_TX ("This mode: receive only")
NXDN, the fit has neither not started (nxdn.begin_fails) refused, TXDENY_REFUSED
No codec on a board that needs one running refused, TXDENY_NO_CODEC
Leaving NXDN, a retune stopped, started again on the new frequency

Transmit timing, the time-out and the release follow P25's (one table of operations per air mode, radio_air.h). Settings sent while transmitting take effect when the transmission ends; a rate change starts the engine over.

6.4 Scan

A zone scan (or a VFO scan in NXDN) stops on a stored NXDN channel as on any other: the tune starts the engine at the channel's rate. As P25's (p25.md 7.1): nothing heard moves on after the dwell; a carrier (the noise reading or the signal strength) probes up to 700 ms for a call of the channel's RAN; such a call probes up to 1.5 s while its squelch waits for the VCALL (late entry: from the SACCH superframe); a call that plays holds; one that doesn't (another group, encrypted) moves on. A channel at a rate the board's provider can't receive (radio_nxdn_receives: a transmit only fit) is never a stop.

7. The engine (core/nxdn/nxdn_engine.c)

nxdn_engine is NXDN on a channel: the call layer on an air interface session, as p25_engine is P25's (p25.md 6). The settings are those of 6 plus silence, the hooks rx_start, rx_update, rx_voice, rx_end and tx_voice. A call that is not played is still tracked: the view says a call is on the channel, from whom, to whom, encrypted or not. nxdn_engine_set changes the RAN and squelch while running; the call in hand is judged again at its next voice frame.

Transmit follows p25.md 6: prepare (preamble and header queued), the radio core keys, go, voice pulled as the queue drains (a frame not ready is waited for while the queue holds more than the provider's lead plus a chunk, then silence goes), stop (the frame on air finished, the release frame), AIR_TX_DONE, clear.

Memory on Cortex-M4 (-Os):

Item Bytes
nxdn_engine_t (session, call layer both ways, frame buffers) 1104
of it nxdn_call_rx_t / nxdn_rx_t / the decoders' memory 488 / 416 / 384
nxdn_call_tx_t 82
Code: frame codec, receive, call layer, engine, Viterbi 1658 + 615 + 1659 + 1054 + 712
Deepest receive stack below the framer about 310 (call layer 80, field decoder 72, Viterbi 160)

The radio core needs from a board: a provider (board_t.air) whose fit takes 2400 or 4800 symbols/s with a 10- and a 19-symbol sync, and its vocoder (hal_vocoder_t) for the 72-bit frames (6).

They skip where the fit has no RX. Nothing measures the sound at the speaker
tools/bench/nxdntool.py selftest the bench tool (below) both ways at both rates, with bit errors and through the soft modem, from bench receiver records, the summary line

The soft modem test (8 kHz tap, 2000 counts per kHz, so the outer level is 2100 counts and the step between levels 1400): every call whole up to 300 counts of noise; at 400 and 500 one call in eight misses its header and is joined late; at 600 three in eight are whole; every call is still found up to 700. The voice bits there are raw channel bits: their own FEC is the vocoder's.

tools/bench/nxdntool.py (built from tools/bench/nxdn/) runs the firmware's code on the host: gen writes a call as symbols with an expect file, mod turns symbols into discriminator samples, decode and disc read them back through the framer (and the soft modem) and compare. decode reads what p25tool.py decode reads: a dibit file, the bench receiver's records (--records) or rfoundry_debug.py air rx records (--air-jsonl), and ends with a summary: RAN, source, destination, call type, frames, voice frames, CRC failures. Voice is made-up patterns, or with gen --voice-file FILE the 72-bit frames of a recording as they came off the air (9 bytes each): the tool never looks inside a voice frame.

An over checked off air: the radio in NXDN on a test frequency, the SDR bench's 4FSK receiver on it (2400 baud at 4800 bit/s, 4800 baud at 9600 bit/s, root raised cosine, the FSW CDF59 as a 10-symbol sync), its symbols through nxdntool.py decode --records (test_nxdn_tx_off_air).
